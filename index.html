The literature is organised around five connected themes. Themes 1–3 are currently populated with papers verified from the full text; Themes 4–5 will be added as the review progresses.

How the map is used

Papers are grouped by the research problem they help explain rather than by publication chronology. The matrix separates rendering quality, reconstruction supervision, geometric output, and scene setting so that visually convincing novel-view synthesis is not automatically treated as evidence of reliable three-dimensional structure. Each Task 04 topic sentence is developed from multiple papers wherever the literature supports a shared claim.
